# AilaBank — AI Stablecoin Bank

AilaBank is an AI-native, voice-first stablecoin bank that standardizes any-currency value to USDC on Arc, routes cross-border payments, auto-sweeps idle funds to the best safe yield, and gives merchants “zero-MDR” settlements via yield-share — all with on-chain transparency and instant global liquidity.

## What’s different (Blue Ocean)
- Eliminate: physical banking, cards, hidden FX, complex KYC, manual money moves
- Reduce: glam branding, gamified UX, MDR reliance, fiat rail dependence
- Raise: transparency, liquidity, voice UX, best-rate routing, proof-of-best-execution
- Create: RateSweep engine, in‑flight yield, corridor-aware routing, public reliability/cost dashboard

## Core features (MVP scope)
- Cross-border payments: any-currency in → USDC core → any-currency out; corridor-aware PSP/off-ramp routing with visible fees/ETAs
- Store of value: inflation-aware, always-liquid USDC account with AI-managed diversified yield and instant buffer
- Merchant and retail payments: accept USDC with on-chain receipts, invoices/subscriptions/refunds, yield-offset settlement (“zero-MDR” via micro-yield)
- Treasury and cash management for businesses: laddered stablecoin/RWA, policy rules, supplier autopay, audit-ready receipts

## Strategic guarantees
- Best-Rate Guarantee + Proof-of-Best-Execution: show quote, route, and spread for every conversion/transfer
- Always-liquid with buffer; earn-in-transit while funds wait
- Programmable compliance: jurisdiction-aware KYC/KYB, sanctions, Travel-Rule payloads, corridor policy packs

## 90‑day build plan (high level)
- MVP: RateSweep on Arc (USDC), liquidity buffer, best-exec receipts, voice commands, 2 remittance corridors, 1 merchant pilot
- Partners: 1–2 sponsor banks/EMIs, 2 off-ramps per corridor, 1 tokenized T‑bill + 1 DeFi market
- KPIs: all‑in cost on $200 < 3%; median delivery < 5 min; effective merchant MDR ≤ 0.3%
